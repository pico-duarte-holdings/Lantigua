// Operator Profile

Built in the field, not the classroom.

MBA 20+ Yrs Service Career Recruiter Multi-Entity Operator Palm Beach FL

Angel Lantigua is a military leader with more than 20 years of service and a career as a professional recruiter. He holds an MBA and operates multiple businesses under Pico Duarte Holdings LLC — the parent company of Lantigua & Co.

Lantigua & Co. helps small businesses, veteran-owned firms, startups, and growing teams strengthen operations, improve online visibility, and hire and retain the right people. No theory for theory's sake: disciplined systems, honest counsel, and measurable follow-through.

Palm Beach, Florida — South Florida on-site, remote nationwide
