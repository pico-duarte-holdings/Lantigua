Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026
01

Who We Are

This Privacy Policy describes how Lantigua & Co. ("we," "us"), a consulting firm and subsidiary of Pico Duarte Holdings LLC, based in the Palm Beach, Florida area, collects and uses personal information through https://lantigua.net.

02

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, and your message. We do not collect payment information through this website.

03

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry, evaluate whether our services fit your needs, and deliver consulting services if you become a client. We may also use your contact details for follow-up communication directly related to your inquiry.

04

We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate this website and process form submissions (such as our form-handling and email providers), or where required by law.

05

Data Retention

We retain inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and, for clients, for the duration of the engagement plus the period required for business records and legal compliance. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 8).

06

Cookies and Analytics

This website may use basic cookies and privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic (pages visited, general region, device type). We do not use this data to identify you personally. You can block cookies in your browser settings without losing access to the site.

07

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing support@lantigua.net. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and honor applicable rights under Florida and U.S. law.

09

Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults and business use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision, and material changes will be posted on this page.

11

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@lantigua.net or 786-498-0991. Lantigua & Co., a Pico Duarte Holdings LLC company, Palm Beach, Florida.
